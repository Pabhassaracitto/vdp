| English concept | Japanese rendering | Rationale |
|----------------|-------------------|-----------|
| characteristic (lakkhaṇa) | 特相 (tokusō) | Standard Abhidhamma commentary term |
| function (rasa) | 作用 (sayō) | 味 is also used but 作用 is clearer in modern Japanese |
| manifestation (paccupaṭṭhāna) | 現起 (genki) | Standard commentary usage |
| proximate cause (padaṭṭhāna) | 近因 (kin'in) | Standard commentary usage |

### Rejected alternatives

| Term | Reason rejected |
|------|----------------|
| 縁起 (used for Paṭiccasamuppāda as whole) | Cannot reuse the whole concept as a single link name |
| 行 as 行為 | Too colloquial; glossary term 行 is the correct Abhidhamma rendering |
| 愛 as 愛情 | Mahāyāna emotional sense; Theravāda DO context means craving/thirst |
| 取 as 執取 | 取 alone is the glossary term; 執取 adds unnecessary prefix |

### Mahāyāna term rejections

| Term | Context | Rejected because |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 因縁 (causes and conditions in Mahāyāna) | General Buddhist usage | In Mahāyāna 因縁 can mean the general interdependence of all phenomena. In Theravāda Paṭṭhāna context, each of 24 conditions has precise technical meaning. Kept the glossary term 因縁 for Hetu-paccaya specifically. |
| 縁 (en) generically | All conditions | Theravāda Paṭṭhāna requires precise enumeration of what conditions what. Cannot use vague 縁 without specifying the conditioning/conditioned states. |

## 4. Cetasikas (心所)

### Four-aspect terminology

For the 52 cetasikas, the four-aspect structure follows:
- 特相 (lakkhaṇa) = characteristic
- 作用 (rasa) = function  
- 現起 (paccupaṭṭhāna) = manifestation
- 近因 (padaṭṭhāna) = proximate cause

**Confidence level:** `glossary` — terms are from the agreed glossary; prose is composed following the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha framework. | 慧 (Mahāyāna: prajñā as transcendental wisdom) | 慧根 for paññā | Theravāda paññā is a cetasika, one of the 25 beautiful cetasikas |
